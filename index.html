The details

Questions about Pren.

Which states do you cover?

Every US base state. Pren prepares your returns from each state's rules, and your Pren team files them at your base state's portal. Owner-operators and fleets of up to 49 trucks can start on their own; fleets of 50 or more are reviewed by a person first.

Do I need to change my ELD or fuel card?

No. Pren connects to the ELD and fuel card you already use. If yours has no connection yet, you upload its export and we show you exactly where to find it.

Will you ever ask for my passwords?

No. Your ELD, fuel card and bank connect through the provider's own sign-in, and state agencies give Pren access through their own authorization forms. Pren never asks for a password by email, text or phone. If someone does, it is not us.

Who checks my returns before they are filed?

A licensed preparer on your Pren team reviews each return, and nothing is filed until you approve it. You see the return in one plain sentence, the checks it passed and the records behind every number.

I already use a permit service or a bookkeeper. Can I switch?

Yes. Start with your DOT number and connect your data. Pren prepares the current quarter from your ELD's history and picks up each filing as it comes due. A review of earlier quarters is available and priced before you agree to it.

When is the next IFTA return due?

Third-quarter returns, for July to September, are due Oct 31, 2026. Start now and Pren prepares the quarter from your ELD's history.

What does it cost beyond the plan?

The plan price is Pren's whole fee for the work in your plan. The taxes and agency fees you owe, such as fuel tax, the 2290 tax, UCR and IRP fees, go from your account straight to the agency. Work outside your plan is priced before you agree to it.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month. Cancel any month and take a full export of your records with you.
