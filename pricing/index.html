The details

Questions about Pren.

Is this software or an accounting service?

Pren is an accounting service powered by expert AI agents. They connect your records, prepare the work and follow up on what is still open. A small professional team handles qualified judgment and review. You make the business decisions and give required approvals.

Can I switch from my current provider?

We review your records, explain any cleanup needed, and agree on a handover before starting. You see the price and timing first.

How do you get my company ready for tax filing?

Your books and supporting records stay together throughout the year. The tax workflow includes professional review, required signatures and an authorized filing route. We track acceptance or rejection and keep follow-up open until it is resolved. The exact filings and responsibilities are agreed before service starts.

Which taxes are included?

We confirm the company, tax year and specific filings before you sign. Extra states, countries or past tax years may need a separate price.

What happens as we grow?

When you hire more people, add a company or expand into another country, we review the extra work and agree the price with you. Your records stay together.

Are there costs outside the subscription?

Necessary ledger software is included. Payroll-provider fees, banking and processing charges, government amounts, applicable taxes, and fixed cleanup work are disclosed separately before acceptance.

Am I signing an annual contract?

Plans are monthly. Before you sign, we explain which annual tax work is covered and what happens to your work and records if you leave.
