Getting started

Start with your USDOT number.

  1. Enter your DOT number.

    Pren fills in your company, trucks and authority from FMCSA, and lists every filing you owe for the next 12 months.

  2. Send your ELD and fuel card data.

    Upload your ELD's IFTA report and your fuel card export. We show you exactly where to find them for your ELD and card. Pren never asks for your passwords.

  3. Approve. We file.

    Each return reaches your phone in one plain sentence, with the checks it passed. You tap Approve, your Pren team files it and keeps the agency's receipt.

The details

Questions about Pren.

Which states do you cover?

Every US base state. Pren prepares your returns from each state's rules, and your Pren team files them at your base state's portal. Fleets of up to 100 trucks start on their own; above 100, a person confirms your truck count first.

Do I need to change my ELD or fuel card?

No. Keep the ELD and fuel card you use. You upload your ELD's IFTA report and your fuel card export, and we show you exactly where to find them.

Will you ever ask for my passwords?

No. You upload your ELD and fuel card exports, and state agencies give Pren access through their own authorization forms. Pren never asks for a password by email, text or phone. If someone does, it is not us.

Who checks my returns before they are filed?

A CPA on your Pren team checks each return, and nothing is filed until you approve it. You see the return in one plain sentence, the checks it passed and the records behind every number.

I already use a permit service or a bookkeeper. Can I switch?

Yes. Start with your DOT number and send your ELD and fuel card data. Pren prepares the current quarter from your ELD's history and picks up each filing as it comes due. A review of earlier quarters is available and priced before you agree to it.

When is the next IFTA return due?

Third-quarter returns, for July to September, are due Mon, Nov 2, 2026 (October 31 falls on a Saturday). Send us your ELD's IFTA report and fuel card export and we build your third quarter.

What does it cost beyond the price?

Nothing. The price is Pren's whole fee: no setup fee, no data fee, no per-filing fee. The taxes and agency fees you owe, such as fuel tax, the 2290 tax, UCR and IRP fees, go from your account straight to the agency. Where we don't run driver payroll yet, you keep your payroll service and pay $7 less per driver each month until we do.

Is there a contract?

You can cancel free until you approve your first month, and we take no card or bank details before then. Fleets that sign by December 31, 2026 keep their price as long as they stay and commit to 12 months after that first month; leaving early costs $145, once, and nothing if you sell or close. Your records are yours to export any time.
