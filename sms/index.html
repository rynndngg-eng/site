Texts from Pren.

Fleets using Pren can get questions and approvals about their fuel tax and filings by text. This page shows how a fleet turns texts on, what Pren sends and how to stop.

Last updated Sep 27, 2026.

How a fleet turns texts on

Texts are off until someone agrees to them. To turn them on, the fleet's owner or office manager signs in to the Pren app and adds a mobile number, for themselves or for a driver, and agrees to this statement, shown beside the number:

Pren keeps the words, their version, who agreed and when. A number nobody agreed for is never texted. Agreeing is not a condition of using Pren: every question also comes by email and waits in the app.

What Pren texts

  • Questions only your fleet can answer, such as which truck a fuel purchase belongs to.
  • Receipts to send and returns to approve.
  • Nothing else. No marketing, no promotions and nothing from other companies.

Message frequency varies with your filings. Message and data rates may apply. An example text, for a made-up fleet:

Pren: Red Mesa Freight's fuel card shows 118.402 gal in Amarillo, TX on Sep 12. Which truck? Reply 1 for Truck 12 or 2 for Truck 7. Reply STOP to stop texts, HELP for help.

Stop, start and help

  • Reply STOP at any time and Pren stops texting that number.
  • Reply START to start again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email ryan@usepren.com.
  • The first text Pren sends says how to stop and how to get help.

Privacy and terms

Pren does not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or text-messaging consent for marketing. Read the privacy policy and the terms.
