Payroll and settlements.

Company drivers paid through your payroll provider and reconciled with your books, or paid through Pren for California drivers. Owner-operators get clear settlements and their 1099-NEC, without logging in to anything.

What is included

  • Driver payroll reconciled with your books, or run by Pren for California drivers.
  • Owner-operator settlements and escrow, shown line by line.
  • Forms 1099-NEC for owner-operators at year end.
  • Drivers answer requests by link. They never need an account.
