About Pren

We do the paperwork. You run the trucks.

Pren does the back office for trucking companies, from San Francisco. Software reads your ELD, fuel card and bank, prepares every filing you owe and keeps your books. A licensed preparer on your Pren team reviews the work. You approve, and we file.

Why Pren

Too many vendors. Too much paper.

A small fleet today pays a permit service for fuel tax, a website for the 2290, another for UCR, a bookkeeper and a tax preparer, and still keeps a shoebox of receipts in case of an audit. Each one asks for the same information again.

Pren starts from your DOT number and the data your trucks already make: ELD miles, fuel card gallons, bank and factoring statements. From those it prepares every filing you owe and keeps your books, with the proof behind every number.

How we work

Plain, proven and yours.

Your data, never your passwords.

Your ELD, fuel card and bank connect through their own sign-in. States give Pren access through their own authorization forms.

Every number has a source.

Every figure on a return opens to the miles, gallons or statement behind it.

Reviewed, then approved.

A licensed preparer reviews each return. Nothing is filed until you approve it.

Your records stay yours.

Month to month, no contract. Export your books and records whenever you like.
