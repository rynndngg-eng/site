About Pren

We do the accounting. You run the company.

Pren is an accounting service for startups, based in San Francisco. Expert AI agents connect your records, prepare the work and follow up on what is still open. A small professional team handles qualified judgment and review. You make the business decisions and give the approvals that need you.

Why Pren

Too much time on accounting.

Receipts, spreadsheets and deadlines compete with the company you are building. Founders end up managing the accounting as well as the company.

Pren takes over the books, business taxes, payroll and cash planning in one service. You get current books, clear cash numbers, and more time for customers, product and hiring.

How we work

Clear, reviewed and agreed up front.

Agreed before we start.

You see the included work, the full price and the first delivery date before you accept.

Every number has a source.

Transactions, supporting records and corrections stay together, and answers show the records and calculations behind them.

Professional review.

Pren prepares. You approve. Accountants review. Required signatures come before anything is filed.

Your records stay yours.

Export your books and supporting records whenever you need them, and move providers with your history intact.
