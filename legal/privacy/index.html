Privacy.

Last updated September 24, 2026.

This website

usepren.com uses PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how the site is used and to find problems. It collects the pages you view, the links and buttons you click, errors that happen in your browser, and a replay of your visit: what was on screen and where you scrolled and clicked. Anything typed into a form field is masked before it leaves your browser.

PostHog sets a first-party cookie containing a random visitor ID so that repeat visits from the same browser can be counted together. It does not identify you by name or email unless you later sign in to the Pren app. Your IP address is used to estimate your approximate location and is not stored with the events. Data is processed by PostHog Inc. in the United States. Session replays are kept for 30 days.

There are no advertising pixels on this website. Fonts and scripts other than PostHog are served with the site. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to operate and secure the site.

To opt out, block scripts from posthog.com in your browser or ad blocker, or email zoe@usepren.com to have data linked to your visitor ID deleted.

The plan builder on the home page keeps your choices in memory in your browser tab. Nothing from it is submitted until you choose to continue in the Pren app.

The Pren app

When you choose Get started or Sign in, you move to app.usepren.com. The app uses the information you provide, such as your name, email address, company details and connected financial records, to create your account and deliver the service you agree to. How your company's records are handled is set out in the service agreement you review and accept in the app before any work starts.
